About this prototype. Add Vantage analyzes your photo through a private backend — no API key to set up, nothing to paste in. Take a photo, record a slow pan of a scene, or choose an existing photo/video from your library. Add this page to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) for quick access. This is a small private beta, not a public App Store app.
